# Spring Plant Sale

Annual fundraiser selling seedlings and cuttings.

The sale runs on the first Saturday of May in the village hall car park. Prices are illustrative: seedlings 1.50, herbs 2.00, tomato plants 2.50.

Seedlings come from the [[Seed Starting Calendar]] schedule. Proceeds fund the tool pool, see [[Club Meeting Notes 2026-03]].
