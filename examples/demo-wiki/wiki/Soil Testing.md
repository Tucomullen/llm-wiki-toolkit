# Soil Testing

Simple pH and texture tests members can do themselves.

Buy a pH kit from the club store. Take samples from 10 cm depth at five spots per bed and mix them.

Most vegetables like a pH between 6.0 and 7.0. For texture, do the jar test: shake soil with water and let it settle; sand, silt and clay form separate layers. Results are recorded in the [[Raised Bed Layout]] plan.
