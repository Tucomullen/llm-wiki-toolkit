# Herb Garden Guide

Growing basil, thyme, mint and parsley in the herb spiral.

The herb spiral has a dry sunny top for thyme and rosemary, and a damp shady base for mint and parsley. Keep mint in a buried pot, because it spreads.

Basil is sown following the [[Seed Starting Calendar]]. Harvest from the top to keep plants bushy.
