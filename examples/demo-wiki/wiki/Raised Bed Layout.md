# Raised Bed Layout

Dimensions and crop rotation plan for the community raised beds.

The garden has eight beds of 1.2 m by 3 m. Rotate crop families yearly: legumes, then brassicas, then fruiting crops, then roots.

Paths are 60 cm wide so wheelbarrows fit. Fill beds with a mix of topsoil and [[Composting Basics]] output. Mulch with straw as in the [[Mulching Guide]].
