# Tomato Care

Growing tomatoes in the club's raised beds.

Tomatoes need six to eight hours of sun and steady moisture. See [[Watering Schedule]] for how often to water and [[Mulching Guide]] to keep roots cool.

## Planting

Start seeds indoors following the [[Seed Starting Calendar]], then harden off for a week. Bury two thirds of the stem when transplanting: the buried stem grows extra roots.

## Common problems

Blossom end rot usually means uneven watering, not a calcium shortage. Check the soil with the steps in [[Soil Testing]] before adding amendments. For aphids and hornworms see [[Pest Control Basics]].
