# Composting Basics

How the club runs its three-bin compost system.

Our compost uses three bins: fresh, cooking and finished. Aim for roughly three parts brown material (dry leaves, cardboard) to one part green (kitchen scraps, grass clippings).

## Turning

Turn the cooking bin every two weeks. The pile should feel like a wrung-out sponge. If it smells of ammonia, add browns; if it is dry, add water.

Finished compost goes into the [[Raised Bed Layout]] beds each spring. Test the result with [[Soil Testing]].
