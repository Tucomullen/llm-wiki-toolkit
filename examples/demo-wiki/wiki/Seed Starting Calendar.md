# Seed Starting Calendar

When to sow which crops indoors and outdoors.

Dates assume a last frost around mid-May.

| Crop | Sow indoors | Transplant |
|---|---|---|
| Tomato | early March | late May |
| Basil | early April | early June |
| Lettuce | direct sow | April |
