# Mulching Guide

Which mulches the club uses and when to apply them.

Apply 5 cm of straw or leaf mould after the soil has warmed in late spring. Keep mulch a hand's width away from stems to avoid rot.

Mulch suppresses weeds and holds moisture, which reduces the effort in the [[Watering Schedule]]. Use finished [[Composting Basics]] output as a top dressing in autumn.
