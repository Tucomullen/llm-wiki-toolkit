# Watering Schedule

Rotating watering duty and how much water each crop needs.

Volunteers sign up for watering duty on the rota board in the tool shed. Water deeply twice a week rather than lightly every day.

Tomatoes and squash need about 4 litres per plant per watering. Herbs from the [[Herb Garden Guide]] need less. Mulch cuts watering needs by roughly a third; see [[Mulching Guide]]. Tools for watering are in the [[Tool Library Rules]] loan list.
