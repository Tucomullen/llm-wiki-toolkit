# Pest Control Basics

Non-chemical pest control used in the club garden.

We avoid synthetic pesticides. First choice is prevention: healthy soil, crop rotation and row covers.

## Aphids

Spray with a strong jet of water, then release ladybird larvae if the infestation returns. Check [[Tomato Care]] for tomato-specific advice.

## Slugs

Use beer traps and copper tape around lettuce. Water in the morning, as described in [[Watering Schedule]], so leaves dry before night.
