# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>. It applies to all project spaces
(issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where someone represents the project).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Encouraged: empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions, constructive feedback given and
accepted gracefully, taking responsibility for mistakes, focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without
permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject
contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report incidents privately to the maintainers using GitHub's private vulnerability/security reporting
for this repository, or by contacting a maintainer listed in the repository. All reports are reviewed
promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary
ban, permanent ban).
